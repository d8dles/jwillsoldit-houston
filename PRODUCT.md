# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Web.

## Users

People making a Houston-area housing move who need to understand the region before evaluating a specific address. Joey is the editorial operator and maintainer.

## Product Purpose

Houston, Handled. is a practical, source-cited Greater Houston orientation guide. It explains regions, local systems, and address-dependent questions, then gives ready visitors a clear handoff to Smart Move or direct contact.

## Positioning

Structural, neutral orientation without neighborhood ranking or steering. The product explains loops, corridors, jurisdictions, utilities, flood review, taxes, restrictions, and the questions that require address-level verification.

## Operating Context

The customer path is:

`Houston landing → regions, areas, and guides → related editorial links → Smart Move, call, or text`

Content is maintained in Markdown with source access dates, review dates, draft or published status, automated content gates, and human and broker review.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Standalone static Astro application served under `/houston`.
- Current merged inventory: 9 regions, 10 guides, 6 areas, and 27 routes.
- Source, schema, fair-housing language, voice, and content checks are build gates.
- Customer-facing links must resolve to a working destination when published. Do not add speculative or placeholder links.
- Volatile program, availability, rate, or eligibility data belongs only when it can be refreshed from a dependable official source. Otherwise, link to the official source without restating a changing number or current-status claim.
- Schematic maps must retain accuracy captions and must not imply parcel, school-zone, flood-zone, or travel-time precision.
- No community stub pages, location finder, public methodology page, CMS, database, Mapbox, listings feed, or live market data in the current product.
- Houston is editorial orientation. Smart Move remains the intake product; the Hub remains the parent site and routing layer.
- The product does not determine loan eligibility, underwriting, affordability, or a supported purchase price.

## Brand Commitments

- Houston, Handled. is part of JWILLSOLDIT, not a separate brand.
- Joey's voice is direct, practical, calm, and honest about what varies.
- The visible product preserves CRG, TREC, Equal Housing, IABS, and Consumer Protection Notice elements.
- The JWILLSOLDIT wordmark includes its red brand dot and links to the parent site.
- The next step is a clear Smart Move handoff, not a second intake system.
- Exact visual rules live in `DESIGN.md`.

## Evidence on Hand

- Content collections and enforced schemas.
- Per-page source metadata and visible review dates.
- Shared disclaimers placed near relevant claims.
- Schematic-map data and components.
- IABS, Consumer Protection Notice, and supplied CRG logo assets.
- No approved testimonial library, authentic Houston photography system, or researched community inventory beyond the six current area pages.

## Product Principles

- Orient before converting.
- Explain systems; never rank people or places.
- Verify property-dependent facts at the address level.
- Make provenance useful to customers, not an internal-process exhibit.
- Publish working links only; remove or replace broken destinations.
- Prefer self-refreshing official data for facts that change. When automation is unavailable, display a verification date and avoid evergreen wording.
- Separate education from professional determinations.
- Expand only with independently researched content that has a named maintenance path.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The product targets WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, touch-friendly targets, neutral fair-housing language, readable long-form measures, and the required compliance footer.
