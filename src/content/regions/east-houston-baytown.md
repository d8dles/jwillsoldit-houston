East Houston and Baytown follow the Houston Ship Channel through Harris County and toward Chambers County, with I-10, SH 225, SH 146, and Beltway 8 carrying most regional trips. The Ship Channel, port traffic, rail lines, and industry are visible parts of this side of Houston, alongside older postwar neighborhoods, townhouses, newer subdivisions, and acreage farther east. I would visit the immediate block and drive the route you expect to use; truck traffic, rail crossings, drainage, and city boundaries can change quickly from one address to another.
